Which package do I need?

Two packages, one direction of dependency. Behavior never depends on style.
I bring my own stylingHeadless behavior, keyboard, focus and ARIA. No CSS, no tokens.@scnx/core-ui
I want the Scnehaux look most teamsStyled components, themes, tokens and fonts. Installs core-ui as a peer.@scnx/system + @scnx/core-ui
core-ui← peer —system← install —your app
