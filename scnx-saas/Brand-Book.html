Brand bookThe scnx-saas look

Materials on a stage. Ink that acts.

How the Scnehaux SaaS products look: layered materials on a dotted stage, corners that nest, and ink for every action and every mark of place. Every colour, radius and shadow on this page is read from @scnx/system's scnx-saas tokens; switch the mode in the nav.

See the boards Boards and package
This book covers

The look of the SaaS products: materials, the radius ladder, colour roles, the stage, type, space, states, motion, and how the shipped components read under this theme.

It does not cover

The name, the mark, the voice and the icons: those are the brand every theme shares, on the Scnehaux brand page. Token values and component contracts belong to ui-platform.

02 · Signatures

Six things that make a screen read as Scnehaux.

Never drop one to match another library's look. Signatures 1 and 2 are why this theme exists (the industrial structure, item 8).

01

Materials, not boxes

Every container is one of eight materials. A material binds fill, radius, a 1px ring and a shadow as one unit; never hand-mix them.

02

Corners that nest

The ladder 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24. A child's radius is its parent's radius minus the parent's padding, so corners never collide.

03

Ink acts, and marks the place

The primary action is ink. In this theme the primary scheme is ink too, so focus, links, checks and "here" are ink, told apart by their shape: a ring, an underline, a rail, a fill.

04

The stage

Marketing, docs, empty states and previews sit on a tinted ground with a 1px dot every 22px. Product data sits on the plain canvas.

05

The eyebrow and the tight display

Kickers, table heads and metadata are mono, upper case, +0.12em. Headlines are Inter semibold, tracked tight.

06

The rise

Floating materials rise 4px and fade in; pressed controls sink 1px. All of it stops under reduced motion. Guidance until an ADR records it.

Rings, not borders. A container draws its 1px edge inside its shadow, so nesting never shifts layout. One shadow per stack: a raised card inside a panel becomes flat. In dark mode the floating materials (menu, modal, sheet) add a lit top edge, an inset 1px line in neutral.border.default, because shadows read weakly on dark grounds. Controls keep a real 1px border (neutral.border.strong, at least 3:1). Hairlines between rows are inset 1px shadows in neutral.border.subtle. The older ds-effect-shadow-* tokens remain for existing components; new work uses ds-effect-material-*.
04 · Radius

The concentric ladder: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24.

A child's radius is its parent's radius minus the parent's padding. This sheet is the rule at work: a 24 panel padded 8 holds 16 cards; the nav, 16 padded 8, holds 8 items.

The scale, read from the tokens
panel 24 · pad 8
container 16 · pad 8
Row element 8
Row element 8
Do · 24 padded 8 holds 16

The inner corner follows the outer one, so the gap between them is even all the way round.

Don't · the same radius inside

An inner corner as round as its parent pinches the gap at the corners and reads as a collision.

Shapes that mean something stay round in every theme and under every hook: the radio indicator, the switch thumb, the status dot, the spinner (pill). They are anatomy, not hooks.
05 · Colour

An ink palette. Roles, never hues.

The grammar. Schemes: neutral, primary, info, success, warning, danger. Roles: surface, text, icon, border, shadow. States: default, hover, pressed, disabled, selected, focus. Every scale is a 12-step ramp from the palette engine (Display P3, gated at WCAG 2.2), and the role-to-step mapping is the same in both modes, so a component never chooses a token per mode. A solid fill always takes its scheme's contrast label: ink with neutral.text.contrast, danger with danger.text.contrast.

Ink replaces the primary scheme only. Info, success, warning and danger keep their hues, and each still needs a word or an icon: success and danger are never told apart by green and red alone.

06 · Type

One family, and a mono for the eyebrow.

Inter (variable, wght 100–900 and opsz), served by the package, with the single-storey a ("cv11" 1) and optical sizing. Code and eyebrows use the system mono stack.

Ship the interface.

display · Inter 600 · −0.055em · leading 0.95 · fluid with clamp()

Heading large

heading-large · 2rem · 700 · −0.02em (from heading-large up)

Body default for forms and reading. Body small (14px) for dense UI: alerts, menus, tables, descriptions.

body-default 1rem/1.5 · body-small 0.875rem/1.5 · article-readable 1.75 leading

Tokens · colour

eyebrow · code-small mono · 0.75rem · +0.12em · upper case · subtle text

Section kickers, table heads, group labels, metadata.

Labels on buttons and tabs are label-default at semibold or medium; never upper-case a label except through the eyebrow or label-small. Weights stay on the token scale (400, 500, 600, 700). Dashboard numbers use metric-display; tables use data-compact.
