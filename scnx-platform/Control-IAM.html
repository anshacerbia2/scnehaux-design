Control, in six rules.

01

Grey at rest

A panel with nothing wrong is monochrome. The accent is spent only on what needs a person, as a 6px lamp or a hairline, never as a fill or a figure.

02

Annunciate, don't chart

A condition is a window that lights, with a count and what tripped it. The first to trip is first out. Every window opens the station that clears it.

03

Show the path

The hops a request takes, browser to kernel, sit under the strip on every page, so a failure says where before it says what.

04

Arm, then act

A command that changes a person is closed until armed. Armed, it shows what holds it: a reason, a recent sign-in, not yourself, a second operator.

05

Tag what you lock

Containment carries a tag: who, since when, why, and what lifts it. A state is never only a word in a badge.

06

Seal the reads that are recorded

A read that is itself on the record is sealed until broken on purpose. Commands, alarms and broken seals are kept in order, to the millisecond.
