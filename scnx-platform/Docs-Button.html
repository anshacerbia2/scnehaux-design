Props
03

        

          

            
PropTypeDefaultBehavior

            
              
variant'primary' | 'secondary' | 'ghost' | 'danger''primary'Visual weight. Keep one primary per view. Danger only on the confirming action.

              
size'small' | 'medium' | 'large''medium'Control height 28, 36 or 44. Inline padding is half the height minus 6.

              
loadingbooleanfalseShows a spinner, sets aria-busy, ignores clicks and keeps its width.

              
disabledbooleanfalseNative disabled. Prefer explaining nearby why an action is unavailable.

              
iconStart · iconEndReactNode—Decorative icon slots, hidden from assistive technology, sized 16 or 20 by the control size.

              
asChildbooleanfalseRenders your own element, such as a router link, with the same styles and focus ring.

              
fullWidthbooleanfalseFills its container. Useful in sheets on small screens.

            
          

        

      


      
      

        

        
Accessibility contract
04

        

          

            
What the component keeps in every version. Break one and CI stops the release.

            
    
              
  • Tab moves focus to the button
    • 
              
  • EnterSpace activate it
    • 
              
  • ShiftTab moves focus back
    • 
            

          

          

            
Native element<button>
Role, focus and activation come from the platform, not from script.

            
Label contrast–
Label on the ink fill, measured here from the tokens of the current mode.

            
Focus visible–
2px Scnx Blue ring with a 2px offset, shown for keyboard focus.

            
Target size36 × 36
Every size meets the 24px minimum of WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8. Large reaches 44.

            
Busy statearia-busy
Loading is announced once through a polite live region.

            
Reduced motionrespected
The spinner stops; the state is still announced. The press keeps its 1px sink.
