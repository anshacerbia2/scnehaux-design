Three worlds behind one door.

Scnehaux UI is three packages, so its door is three worlds on one screen, in the order a part is made: behaviour, then paint, then movement. One edge runs between two worlds at a time, and the same panel is drawn in each world's material, so a wipe cuts one part in two.

02 The worlds
Count
three
Mode
dark

One panel, three materials.

Each world is a sheet: its number, a claim, the way in and three figures on the left; on the right a bench, the release card drawn as on an engineering drawing, with crop marks, a 380 dimension line, numbered balloons and a title block. Only the material changes, so the card's box is the same in every world at every width.

01 · @scnx/core-ui

Primitives: bare

No fill, shadow or colour of its own. The card's edge is dashed, markup-only parts sit at 30%, and the behaviour's own elements (the segments, the buttons) are outlined dashed in the signal. A focus ring walks the tab order. Ground: graph paper, 88px over 22px, on the sunken surface.

02 · @scnx/system

Design system: painted

The components as published, on the raised surface. The title block is the bill of materials: each part and its page. Ground: the 22px dot grid.

03 · in fabrication

Motion: moving

The card's frame holds still while its content cycles by the motion tokens: out on exit, in on enter, the checks counting 0 to 12. In place of the title block, a gauge draws the five motion roles as three curves, each with a rider. Ground: the 88px squares alone. Says "Coming soon" until the package ships.

03 The light mode
Mode
light

The same drawing on the light ground.

Every value is a token of scnx-platform, so the light mode is the theme's, not a second design: ink for the way in, blue only for here (the current tab's bar, the sheet number, the primitives' outlines).

04 One edge
Wipe
1050 ms
Scan lines
one

Only the edge moves.

The world coming in is on top and clipped from its side; the world it replaces is laid under, whole and still. On the edge, a 1px signal line, a 160px fade behind it and the incoming world's name at its top, seen only while the edge moves. These frames hold the edge by a drag, from Primitives to the Design system.

RuleWhat it holds
One wipeA registered --reveal on the top world transitions over 1050ms by the draw easing; nothing else on the stage moves.
One scan lineAny other wipe under way is cancelled on every change, so there is never more than one line on the screen.
Never starts overChanged mid-wipe, the edge goes on or turns from where it is. It is never put back at an end.
The lockMid-wipe only the two worlds on the stage can be chosen; a third waits, its tab faint and aria-disabled.
05 Moves
Dwell
7 s
Drag
18%

Forward runs left to right. Everything else comes back.

The next world comes in from the left, so the worlds turn 1, 2, 3 the way the page reads. Any other move, a world before or round from 3 to 1, comes in from the right. A drag is the one exception: its edge follows the hand, round the ring.

The ring
InputDoes
RailTabs, manual activation: ← → move, Home and End go to the ends.
KeysOutside a field: ← → step back and on, 1 to 3 go to a world.
WheelFrom lg, one step per gesture after 40px of travel.
DragAfter 8px, more across than down; the edge under the pointer. Let go past 18% of the width, or faster than 0.5px/ms, and it runs on; short of it, it runs back.
AutoplayThe current tab's bar fills over 7s, then the next world comes. It holds for keyboard focus, a drag, the lens or a mode wipe, and goes on from where it paused.
06 The X-ray lens
Square
168 px
Input
mouse
What lies under the paint

A step back, at the same place.

A mouse over the current world's bench opens a 168px square onto the world before it: the primitives under the design system, the design system under motion. Over the first world there is no world before, and the square scans empty ground, named "00 · Nothing before".

It is a frame with ticks at the middle of its sides and the world's name under it, written a frame at a time with no layout. Never for touch or under reduced motion, never during a wipe or a drag; while it is open the worlds do not turn.

07 The mode wipe
Only on

At a time

A change of light is a world of its own.

On the door, light, dark or system is drawn over the old page with the same edge and its own scan line, over 1050ms: into dark from the left, into light from the right. A view transition cannot be caught in a still, so it is drawn here.

Into dark · left to right
Into light · right to left
RuleWhat it holds
After a worldA mode chosen while a world wipes waits for it to land, so the page never shows two edges.
One at a timeA choice made during a wipe is kept, the last only, and run after if it is not what shows by then.
No blendThe two pictures are laid one over the other, never cross-faded: dark over light must not vanish into it.
ElsewhereEvery other page switches its mode at once, as before.

Copy over bench, still one screen.

Below lg the copy stands over the bench. The worlds share the stage's rows, so the bench starts at one height in every world and a wipe still cuts the card in two. The figures leave, then the lede on a short screen; the rail drops its numbers. Only a phone on its side lets the page scroll.

The look is held here. The behaviour stays in the site.

The front door was designed in the site, not drawn here first as D13 asks. This board adopts its look into scnx-platform as built, so the next change to it starts here.

Here, in scnehaux-design

The look

Three worlds, one panel in three materials, one edge with one scan line, the grids, the rail, the lens frame. A change to any of them is drawn on this board first, by pull request, and the site follows.

In ui-platform-docs

The behaviour

Timings, moves, locks, flags and where it is off: annex C of the site's TDD, written from its code. This board quotes it and never overrides it.

From ui-platform

The parts

The card's Badge, Heading, Text, SegmentedControl, Progress, Kbd and Button, and the motion tokens the gauge draws, are @scnx/system's and @scnx/core-ui's, as published.

Frames. Every picture on this board is the site itself: a production build of ui-platform-docs at 1c72bf3 in Chromium, 31 October 2026. The stills are taken under reduced motion, which draws a world at once; the edge is held by a drag; the mode wipe is drawn, since a view transition cannot be caught in a still. Retake them when the door changes.
