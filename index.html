Design system

Scnehaux UI

43 component cards with their usage and their delta from the current build, in both themes and both modes.

Components that carry no design of their own: each reads a hook that falls back to a token the theme maps. Ink is the main action in every theme; scnx-platform draws square with Scnx Blue for "here", scnx-saas draws materials on the ladder 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 in ink.

Designs
